


byte:   standard 8 bits

d word: double word

dq: double quadword

double word: two words, 4 bytes, 32 bits

double quadword:    2 quad words, 8 words, 16 bytes, 128 bits

octo word: double quadword,  8 words, 16 bytes, 128 bits

q word: quad word
quad word: four words, 8 bytes, 64 bits

SSE: Streaming SIMD Extensions

word:   two bytes, 16 bits
